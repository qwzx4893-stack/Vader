# Code of Conduct

We want Vader to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and propose changes without fear of being belittled.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good faith, give specific and kind feedback, accept it graciously, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discrimination of any kind, publishing other people's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the address on their GitHub profile, or open a private security advisory from the repository's **Security** tab if the report is sensitive.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0).
